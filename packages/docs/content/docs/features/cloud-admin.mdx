The admin panel is where you run a curl-runner Cloud server. It lives at `/admin` on your dashboard. Owners and admins see it. Everyone else gets a plain 404, as if the page didn't exist.

See [Cloud](/docs/features/cloud) for what teams and collections are, and [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting#admins) to set up your first owner.

<Callout title="Every change is logged">
Each write in the panel adds a line to the [activity log](#activity), with who did it and when.
</Callout>

## Overview [#overview]

The overview shows how the server is doing: sign-ups and new teams over 30 days, publishes per day, collections, plan overrides, and a status strip for the API, email and sign-up.

## Users [#users]

**Users** lists everyone on the server. Search, then open a person to act on their account.

<CloudScreenshot name="admin-users">The users list, with each person's teams and last sign-in.</CloudScreenshot>

- **Suspend** stops a person signing in and deletes their sessions. They see "This account is suspended. Contact support." Publish tokens they created keep working, so CI doesn't break.
- **Sign out everywhere** deletes the person's sessions. They can sign in again.
- **Delete** removes the account.

You can't delete the sole owner of a team. The panel names the team. Make someone else an owner, or delete the team first.

## Teams and plan overrides [#teams]

**Teams** lists every team with its plan and where the plan comes from. Open a team to see its members, collections and plan history.

<CloudScreenshot name="admin-team">A team with a Team plan granted for 60 days.</CloudScreenshot>

A plan override gives a team a plan without Stripe. Use it for a trial, a partner or a fix.

- An override beats Stripe. While it's in force, the team has the plan you set.
- Stripe keeps billing. An override doesn't cancel a subscription, and the panel warns you when the team pays. Cancel it in Stripe if the grant replaces it.
- An override ends at a date you pick, or never.
- Owners get an email 14 days before it ends, once.
- At the end, the team falls back to its Stripe plan, or to the plan stored on the team if it has no subscription.
- **Remove override** ends it at once. Owners aren't emailed.

Every grant and removal goes in the team's plan history.

You can also suspend a team. Publishing to it gives `403 team_suspended`, its share links stop working, and its members see a notice. Nothing is deleted.

## Collections and link takedown [#collections]

**Collections** lists every collection on the server, with its team, access, versions and size.

<CloudScreenshot name="admin-collections">Every collection on the server. Disable link takes a share link down.</CloudScreenshot>

Disable a share link when someone abuses it. You give a reason, and the team's owners get it by email.

- The link gives 404, and the landing page says "This link no longer works".
- The team keeps the collection and its versions.
- The team can't turn the link back on. A reset gives `403 link_disabled`.
- **Enable link** undoes it.

The **Signed-in opens (30d)** column counts opens by signed-in people only. A person who opens a Public link without signing in isn't counted.

## Emails [#emails]

**Emails** has two tabs: broadcasts and the delivery log.

<CloudScreenshot name="admin-emails">A broadcast draft for the owners of every team.</CloudScreenshot>

A broadcast is a product email to many people. Write it in Markdown. `{{first_name}}`, `{{team_name}}` and `{{plan}}` are filled in per person. Someone in several teams gets one email, with their first team.

Pick an audience:

| Audience | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone | Every user. |
| Team owners | Owners of any team. |
| Paying teams | Owners of teams that pay through Stripe. |
| Free teams | Owners of teams on the Free plan. |
| Granted teams | Owners of teams with a plan override. |

Use **Send test to me** first. **Send** queues one email per person through the outbox, so a restart mid-send doesn't send anyone twice. You can also schedule a send.

Each broadcast has a one-click unsubscribe link. It opens a page that asks for confirmation, so a mail scanner that follows the link can't opt anyone out. People who opt out get no more product emails. Sign-in codes and invites always go out.

The **Delivery log** lists every email the server sent, with its status. Subjects of sign-in emails are masked, so the code never shows.

## Stripe [#stripe]

**Stripe** shows revenue and billing events when billing is on. It has monthly recurring revenue (MRR), a 90-day chart, how teams get the Team plan, and recent subscription events.

- MRR is seats times the price. An annual price counts as a twelfth.
- Teams with an override are counted apart. They aren't revenue.
- Without billing, the page tells you billing is off.

## Health [#health]

**Health** shows API p95 latency over the last 5 minutes, email waiting and failed, Stripe webhooks over 24 hours, when each background job last ran, and rate limits hit in the last hour.

These numbers live in the server's memory. They reset when it restarts.

## Settings [#settings]

**Settings** holds what used to live in the environment.

- Who can create a team: open, allowed domains, or closed.
- The plan for new teams, the member limit and restricted sharing on Free, and the grace period after a failed payment.
- The [banner](/docs/self-hosting#banner).
- [Pause publishing](/docs/self-hosting#pause).

<CloudScreenshot name="admin-settings">Server settings. Changes apply right away.</CloudScreenshot>

Lowering a limit never removes anyone. Only new invites are refused.

A payment-failed email states the deadline using the grace period in force when it was sent. Changing the grace period later doesn't change emails already sent.

The page also lists the environment variables the server reads. It shows names, and values only for URLs, mail host and port, and price IDs. Secrets never show.

## Admins and permissions [#admins]

There are two roles.

- **Owners** come from `OPERATOR_EMAILS`, plus the first account on a server with an empty sign-up list. They have every permission, and you can't remove them in the panel.
- **Admins** you add in **Admins**. Each gets the permissions you pick.

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| Users | Sign people out, suspend and delete accounts. |
| Teams | Suspend teams, change member roles, disable share links. |
| Plans | Grant and remove plan overrides. |
| Emails | Send broadcasts. |
| Settings | Change sign-up, plan limits, the banner and pausing. |
| Admins | Add admins and change permissions. |

Every admin can read every page, including the activity log. Without a permission, the buttons are off and the server answers `403`.

## Activity log [#activity]

**Activity log** lists every change made in the panel: the admin, the action, what it touched and when. Filter it by admin, action or period, or export it as CSV.

<CloudScreenshot name="admin-activity">The activity log, newest first.</CloudScreenshot>

The server keeps entries for 2 years, then deletes them.
