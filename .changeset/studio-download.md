curl-runner studio is now a download: a signed, notarized macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel, attached to each release as `curl-runner-studio-<version>.dmg`. It works with your local YAML files out of the box, and a short walkthrough runs your first request. Sync and sharing are marked as coming soon.

`curl-runner diff a b` now honours `diff.exclude` and `diff.match` from your config file and the file's `global` section.
